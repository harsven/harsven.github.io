Beyond Research

Outside of work, I enjoy traveling, especially when conferences take me to places I would not otherwise have discovered.

I have played table tennis since the age of ten, and I also coached youth teams for several years. During my year at Oxford, I played for the Oxford University Table Tennis Club, and in 2022 our team won the 79th Varsity match against Cambridge.

Games

I also enjoy programming. As part of an introductory course, I developed small games such as Pong and Snake in Python and later ported them to HTML5 and JavaScript, so they run directly in the browser. The code is available on GitHub. Please feel free to explore the code and make any changes you like!

Best played on a desktop computer. Pong and Snake do not support mobile devices.
