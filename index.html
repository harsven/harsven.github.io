Hello! I am a postdoctoral researcher at Trier University and the Institute for Labour Law and Industrial Relations in the European Union (IAAEU). I work on questions in labor economics and political economy, with a preference for using naturally occurring data.

I have also had the opportunity to complete research stays at the University of Oxford (academic year 2021/22) and at the Social Research Institute at University College London (Fall 2025).
